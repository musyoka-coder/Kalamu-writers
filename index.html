How it works

Try the writing workflow without payment

  1. Create an account

    Register for free. If email confirmation is enabled, confirm your address before signing in.

  2. Browse projects

    See Starter, Standard and Premium project briefs and illustrative project amounts.

    Claim projects available to your assigned practice tier and submit writing against each brief.

Project tiers

Review standards

What administrators will assess

Your writing

  • Original text addressing the brief
  • Required word count and clear structure
  • Evidence handling and factual accuracy
  • Professional presentation and readability

Practice limitations

  • Registration and practice do not require payment.

  • Tier upgrades require an administrator to assign a higher package.

FAQ

Practice project access

Is registration free?

Yes. No registration payment or M-Pesa message is required for this practice phase.

Are these paid projects?

No. These are practice assignments with illustrative amounts for evaluating the platform. Submissions do not create earnings.

Can I view higher-tier projects?

Yes. All active learners can view all project briefs. Claiming higher-tier projects requires an administrator-assigned higher tier.

Can I claim a project immediately?

Once registered and signed in, active Starter users can claim eligible Starter practice assignments, subject to their daily claim limit.

How are submissions tracked?

Your dashboard records claimed projects, submissions and feedback. Administrators can view claim activity and editorial decisions.
