How it works

From registration to payment in four steps

  1. Register

    Create your account and pay the registration fee for the package you choose.

  2. Claim a task

    Open your dashboard and claim tasks, up to the daily limit of your package.

  3. Write and submit

    Write to the brief, reach the required word count and submit from any phone or computer.

  4. Get paid

    When your assignment is approved, your earnings are added at your package rate per word.

Questions

Answers before you register

How much will I be paid?

You are paid by the word for each approved assignment. Multiply the word count by the rate of your package: Starter, Standard or Premium. The calculator above shows examples.

When is a task approved?

After you submit, your assignment is reviewed against the brief, the word count and the standards listed above. Approved work is added to your earnings. Work that is not approved is not paid.

How many tasks can I claim?

Each package has a daily limit. Starter has the fewest tasks a day, Standard has more, and Premium has the most. You claim tasks yourself from the Tasks tab of your dashboard.

What do I pay to register?

Registration is KES 500 for Starter, KES 1,500 for Standard and KES 3,000 for Premium. Choose one when you register.

How do I see what I have earned?

Your dashboard has an Earnings tab that lists each submission, its word count, the amount and its status. Your available balance is shown at the top.
